## 🚀 How to Submit to Google News

### Step 1: Wait for Content Maturity
**IMPORTANT:** Google News requires:
- **90 days of consistent publishing** (daily articles)
- **At least 50-100 quality articles**
- **Established readership** (traffic from Google Search)

**Current Status:** You need to publish consistently for 3 months before applying.

### Step 2: Prepare Your Application

1. **Go to Google Publisher Center:**
   - Visit: https://publishercenter.google.com/
   - Sign in with your Google account

2. **Add Your Publication:**
   - Click "Add Publication"
   - Enter: `edensnews.com`
   - Select publication type: "News"
   - Select primary language: English (or Kannada)

3. **Fill Out Publication Details:**
   - **Name:** Edens News
   - **Description:** Multilingual news portal covering Karnataka, India, and world news
   - **Categories:** News, Politics, Sports, Business, Entertainment, Technology
   - **Languages:** English, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Malayalam
   - **Country:** India
   - **Contact Email:** contact@edensnews.com
   - **Address:** Your office address in Bangalore

4. **Verify Ownership:**
   - Add HTML meta tag to your homepage OR
   - Upload verification file OR
   - Use Google Search Console verification

5. **Submit Sitemap:**
   - Add: `https://edensnews.com/.netlify/functions/sitemap`

6. **Wait for Review:**
   - Review takes 2-4 weeks
   - Google will email you with decision

### Step 3: After Approval

Once approved:
- Your articles will appear in Google News
- Monitor performance in Publisher Center
- Keep publishing quality content daily
- Respond to any policy violations quickly
